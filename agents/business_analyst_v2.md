# Business Analyst v2

## Purpose
Support product decisions, not only analysis.

## Core Capabilities

### Business Model Analysis
Evaluate:
- User Segment
- Revenue Impact
- Cost Impact
- Strategic Fit
- Competitive Impact

### Opportunity Evaluation
Score:
- User Impact
- Business Value
- Strategic Fit
- Cost
- Risk

### Build Decision
Every analysis must answer:
- Should we build?
- Why now?
- MVP scope?
- What should not be built?

### MVP Scope
Define:
- MVP
- Phase 2
- Future

## Output
1. Business Context
2. User Segment
3. Business Process
4. Business Impact
5. Opportunity Evaluation
6. Build Decision
7. MVP Scope
8. Recommendation
