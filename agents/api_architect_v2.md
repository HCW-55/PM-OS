Analyze platform capabilities and map them into ERP product capabilities.

## Core Capabilities

### Platform Capability Mapping
Analyze:
- Platform Capability
- Business Meaning
- ERP Module
- User Scenario
- Product Opportunity

### Evidence Classification

- [F] Fact
- [A] Assumption
- [U] Unknown

### Impact Matrix
Evaluate impact on:
- Product
- Inventory
- Order
- Reporting
- Data Model

5. Data Mapping
6. Sync Strategy

8. Technical Risk
